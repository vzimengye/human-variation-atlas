 5.  Estimating trees from DNA sequences 
 a.  Parsimony 最少突变 :  find the tree that requires the fewest possible number of mutations. 
 b.  Probabilistic methods ⽤进化模型找最可能的树   (Likelihood and Bayesian): Model the evolution of 
 DNA sequences and use statistical principles to find the tree with most statistical support. Similar to 
 parsimony but can take biases of mutational process and multiple mutations on a lineage into account 
 c.  Distance based methods ⽤序列差异拼树 :  calculate the number of mutational differences between all 
 pairs of sequences and puzzled together the tree. 
 d.  e.g. Human-chimp sequence divergence: 100 differences; Human-baboon sequence divergence: 400 
 differences; Human-baboon divergence time: 25 million years 
 i.  mutation rate: μ=d/2t=400/(2×25×10^6)=8×10^(−6) 
 ii.  human-chimp divergence time: t=d/2μ=100/(2×8×10^(−6))=6.25×10^6

 13.  Principal Component Analysis (PCA) 
 a.  Extracts different features of the data, called principal components (PCs). (~axes of variation) 
 b.  First PC (PC1)  : explain as much of the variance among individuals as possible. 
 c.  Second PC (PC2)  : explain as much of the variance as possible (not explained by the PC1, etc.) 
 d.  Researchers often focus mostly on the first two PCs. Each individual can be assigned a value 
 corresponding to each of these two PCs. 
 e.  Warnings: 
 i.  sensitive to sample sizes. What you put in is what you get out. 
 ii.  A single XY plot reflects just some of the variation. 
 iii.  Does not explain the causes of variation (population divergence, 
 gene-flow, etc).
